🎯 Difficulty Guide

  • EasySolved almost entirely by spotting cells that are the only option left in a region, row or column.
  • MediumAdds simple elimination: mark ✕ where a queen is impossible, then read off the forced cells.
  • HardNeeds multi-region reasoning — N regions locked into N rows/columns shut other regions out.
  • IntensiveDense chains of deduction, including killer logic: a queen here would starve another region, row or column.
  • Advanced ★Deliberately deep and slow to open. May require lookahead (trial & contradiction) — such boards are tagged with a dot, e.g. "advanced."
  • Master ★★Guaranteed 100% pure logic — never needs trial & error — but forces the deepest techniques (Group Lock / Region Confinement). The fairest hardest level.
  • CustomYou set board size and how many single-cell regions are allowed.

A dot after the badge (e.g. intensive.) means that board cannot be finished by pure step-by-step logic and needs a trial-and-contradiction step.
