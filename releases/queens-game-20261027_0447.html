🎯 Difficulty Guide

  • EasyTier 0–2 only: the last free cell of a region/row/column, plus single-region line exclusion (a region lying in one row or column clears the rest of that line).
  • MediumEverything in Easy, plus at least one multi-region exclusion: 2+ regions confined to the same 2+ rows/columns shut every other region out of them. No killer logic.
  • HardNeeds Tier 3 Killer logic (a queen here would leave some region, row or column with no cell) — but only a few times (fewer than ⌈n/3⌉ deep steps).
  • IntensiveKiller logic is the backbone: at least ⌈n/3⌉ Tier-3 deductions, nothing deeper than Tier 3.
  • Advanced ★Needs Tier 4 or deeper at least once — Zone/Overflow or Group Lock — without reaching the Master density bar.
  • Master ★★Tier 5 Group Lock plus dense chains: ≥35% of n deep steps, ≥70% of n Tier-2+ steps, first queen delayed ≥30% of n steps.
  • CustomYou set board size, region shapes and how many single-cell regions are allowed.

Each level is checked by the built-in step-by-step solver and must land in exactly one class. Every board in every mode is solvable by pure logic — trial and error is never required.
